Development-stage draft. This policy will be reviewed and updated before the public release once the implemented advertising, in-app purchase, analytics, and other SDK behaviors have been verified. It is not a final representation of the release build's data practices.

The game uses on-device storage for settings, progress, and unlocked content. Any network transfers or external storage in the release build will be documented following final verification.

For questions about this policy, email eta.games.japan2026@gmail.com, or visit our support page. This notice will be updated as the app's features and service providers are finalized.
